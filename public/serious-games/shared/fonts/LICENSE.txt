Vendored web fonts of the serious games
=======================================

The .woff2 files in this directory are the `latin` and `latin-ext` subsets of two families, as
served by Google Fonts and copied here so that no pupil's browser asks a third party for them:

- Cormorant Garamond (Christian Thalmann, Catharsis Fonts) - a variable font: one file covers the
  weights 500 and 600 of each style.
- IBM Plex Mono (IBM Corp.) - weights 400, 500 and 600.

Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org).
The OFL is permissive and imposes nothing on the AGPL licensing of the application; the fonts must
not be sold on their own and keep their reserved font names.
